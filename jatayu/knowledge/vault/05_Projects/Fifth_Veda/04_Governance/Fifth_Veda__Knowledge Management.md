# Knowledge Management

## Knowledge Lifecycle
The base content unit of knowledge is the Clip: one scripted, produced, and published short-form piece, belonging to exactly one pillar and built inside exactly one framework[cite: 18]. 

## Source of Truth Philosophy
Each Clip's permanent record links four vital components together:
1.  The research brief[cite: 18].
2.  The approved script[cite: 18].
3.  The locked Ingredient set used for its visuals[cite: 18].
4.  The published asset references across each distribution platform[cite: 18].

These four components are retained together as a single addressable unit[cite: 18]. This ensures that any future revisit of the topic starts from a complete prior record rather than partial fragments[cite: 18].

## Knowledge Maintenance & Correction
Corrections to a published claim are logged directly against the original topic record, and are never handled as an undocumented silent edit[cite: 18].
