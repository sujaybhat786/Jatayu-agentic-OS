# Decision Framework

## Human vs. AI Decision Ownership
The integration of AI tools accelerates execution, but JATAYU does not possess final decision authority over strategy or integrity.
*   **AI Responsibilities:** Execute research gathering, draft scripts against doctrine, generate/lock visual references, run automated quality passes, and surface findings/failures for human review[cite: 18].
*   **Human Responsibilities:** Set topic priority and pillar balance, approve research briefs, make final judgment calls on governance edge cases, and own brand-identity decisions to catch visual/tonal drift[cite: 18].

## Approval Gates
Four approval gates require explicit human sign-off and are never fully delegated to AI[cite: 18]:
1.  The research brief (before scripting)[cite: 18].
2.  The Weapon Line (before the rest of the script is finalised)[cite: 18].
3.  The locked visual Ingredients (before volume shot generation begins)[cite: 18].
4.  The final assembled piece (before publishing)[cite: 18].

## Governance Escalation
Any process that requires manual, non-repeatable judgment at a high volume is flagged[cite: 18]. It becomes a candidate for tightening into an explicit rule and being formally added to this governance manual, rather than being left as tacit knowledge held by one person[cite: 18].
