# AI Governance

## Responsible AI Usage & Boundaries
JATAYU acts as a high-velocity production multiplier, but its deployment is strictly governed by evidentiary and ethical boundaries:
*   **No Hallucinated Sourcing:** AI models execute research gathering against the defined source hierarchy and surface findings for human approval; they must never self-approve or hallucinate sources[cite: 18].
*   **Script Integrity:** AI models draft scripts against the structural doctrine and must flag any topic where the required evidence threshold cannot be met[cite: 18]. AI is forbidden from fabricating comparison points or generating unsourced statistics to fulfill narrative requirements[cite: 18].

## Ethical Boundaries
*   **Visual Generation Limitations:** AI must never generate speculative or imagined likenesses to represent real, named historical figures[cite: 18]. Artefacts, carvings, manuscripts, and landscapes must be used to represent historical persons instead[cite: 18].

## Human Oversight Checkpoints
The AI system operates autonomously only *between* established human approval gates[cite: 18]. At four critical junctures (Research Brief, Weapon Line, Locked Visual Ingredients, and Final Assembly), AI workflows pause entirely until an authorized human grants explicit sign-off[cite: 18].
