# Change Management

## The Living Record
The Governance manual is a living record[cite: 18]. When a genuinely new, permanent operating decision is made, it is added to the relevant section of the master documentation, not left in a separate temporary note[cite: 18].

## Document Revisions & Knowledge Updates
*   **Corrections:** If an error is identified in published content, the correction is logged against the original topic record[cite: 18]. It is not handled as an undocumented silent edit[cite: 18].
*   **Re-evaluations:** If a topic is revisited in the future for a platform re-cut or an update, production begins from the permanently retained research brief, script, and locked visual ingredients rather than re-researching from zero[cite: 18].

## Rule Crystallization
Tacit knowledge must be aggressively converted into explicit documentation. If an edge case requires human judgment repeatedly, it must be formulated into a hard rule and committed to this Knowledge Operating Manual[cite: 18].
