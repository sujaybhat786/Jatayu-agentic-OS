# Folder Architecture

## Project Organization Philosophy
The architecture is designed to scale in content volume without needing structural revision[cite: 18]. New Clips and new sources are simply added as records within the existing schema[cite: 18].

## Core Folder Groupings
Clips are grouped by pillar for discovery purposes, and by Season for scheduling purposes[cite: 18]. These are two independent groupings of the same underlying unit[cite: 18]. 
*   **Seasons:** A Season is a fixed publishing period grouping content across all pillars[cite: 18]. Seasons do not reset the topic taxonomy or the pillar architecture[cite: 18].
*   **Pillars:** A topic's pillar and framework classification is permanent and independent of which Season it was produced in[cite: 18].

## Document Placement Rules
Temporary, topic-specific, or season-specific information is never added to the central Operating Manual or Foundation packs[cite: 18]. It belongs strictly in the individual Clip or Season records[cite: 18].
