# Expansion Strategy

## Adding New Series or Pillars
A new series or sub-series is added by first confirming it fits inside one of the six existing pillars[cite: 18]. If it genuinely does not, the addition of a seventh pillar must be the correct response, decided deliberately rather than by accretion[cite: 18]. A new series never bypasses the research, script, or visual doctrine established in the core operating manuals[cite: 18]. Doctrinal consistency across the whole library is a higher priority than any individual series' creative distinctiveness[cite: 18].

## Adding New Narrative Frameworks
A new narrative framework is introduced only when an existing topic cannot be well-served by any of the six existing frameworks after genuine attempts to fit it[cite: 18]. The bar for adding a framework is high, because framework proliferation degrades the consistency that makes the content library legible as a single coherent system[cite: 18]. A proposed new framework must specify its governing logic and the pillar(s) it serves before it is adopted[cite: 18].

## Integrating New AI Tools
A new production tool—for research, scripting, or visual generation—is adopted by mapping it onto the existing workflow stage it replaces or augments, not by redesigning the workflow around the tool's specific capabilities[cite: 18]. The doctrine is tool-independent by design; any new tool is expected to conform to the doctrine, rather than the doctrine being revised to suit a new tool's default behaviour[cite: 18].
