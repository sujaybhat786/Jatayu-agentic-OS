# Counterargument Policy

## Active Discovery
Every topic is actively checked for credentialed scholarly disagreement before it is scripted[cite: 6]. Research must explicitly search for terms like '[topic] debunked' or 'scholarship against [topic]'[cite: 4].

## Handling Opposing Viewpoints
*   **Neutral Presentation:** If a live scholarly dispute exists, it must either be addressed directly in the script, or the thesis must be reframed to stay inside what is uncontested[cite: 6].
*   **Academic Fairness:** The system must not hide known counterarguments. Hiding a counterargument is treated as a severe content governance violation, not a stylistic choice[cite: 6]. 
*   **Evidence Weighting:** If strong counterarguments exist from credentialed academics and cannot be logically addressed with primary evidence, the claim must be weakened, reframed, or abandoned[cite: 4].
