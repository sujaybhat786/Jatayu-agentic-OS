# Primary Sources (Tier 1)

## Accepted Primary Sources
Primary sources are the bedrock of The Fifth Veda. Every claim must trace back to a primary source[cite: 4].

*   **Ancient Texts:** Original Sanskrit, Pali, Tamil, or other primary-language manuscripts and texts[cite: 6]. 
*   **Archaeology & Historical Records:** Official government archaeological reports, museum archives, and documented historical records[cite: 4, 6].

## Specificity Requirements
It is unacceptable to vaguely claim "the text says." Research must identify the exact Sanskrit or original reference[cite: 4]. This includes:
*   The specific shloka[cite: 4].
*   The verse number[cite: 4].
*   The chapter or Parva (if applicable)[cite: 4].
*   The specific manuscript reference[cite: 4].

## Authorized Repositories
Research should prioritize established digital and physical archives:
*   **Vedic/Upanishadic Content:** The Vedabase, the Bhandarkar Oriental Research Institute digital archive, or University of Chicago's Digital South Asia Library[cite: 4].
*   **Historical/Archaeological Content:** Archaeological Survey of India (ASI) reports, JSTOR, and university press publications[cite: 4].
