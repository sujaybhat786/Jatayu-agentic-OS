# Citation Standards

## Citation Philosophy
The Fifth Veda names sources on screen and in captions as a matter of routine, treated as a competitive advantage rather than defensive overhead[cite: 6]. Sourcing is generated from the research package at the point of scripting, not reconstructed after the fact[cite: 6].

## Source Attribution (Visual & Textual)
Every piece of content carries its sourcing in two places[cite: 6]:
1.  **Visual Citations:** A brief on-screen citation overlay appears at the exact moment the strongest claim is made[cite: 6].
2.  **Textual Disclosure:** A full source reference list is provided in the accompanying caption or description[cite: 6].

## Evidence Disclosure & Historical Uncertainty
All claims must be traceable[cite: 6]. If historical uncertainty exists (e.g., competing dates for a manuscript), the citation must reflect the most rigorously sourced academic consensus or clearly state the range of uncertainty[cite: 6].
