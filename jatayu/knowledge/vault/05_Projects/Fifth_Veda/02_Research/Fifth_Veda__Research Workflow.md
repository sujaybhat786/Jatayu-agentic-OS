# Research Workflow

Research proceeds in a fixed, unalterable order[cite: 6]. 

### 1. Research Request & Assignment (Phase 0)
*   Receive the approved topic, title, audience type, and hook frame[cite: 4].
*   Write a one-sentence central thesis stating what the episode will prove[cite: 4].

### 2. Evidence Collection (Phase 1 & 2)
*   **Primary Source:** Locate and log the primary source with its exact reference (verse, page, archive record)[cite: 6].
*   **Secondary Source:** Complete an academic validation pass against peer-reviewed literature[cite: 6]. Minimum 3 papers[cite: 4].
*   **Expert Voices:** Identify 2-3 credentialed experts and usable quotes[cite: 4].

### 3. Verification & Validation (Phase 2 & 4)
*   **Western Equivalent Search:** Identify the Western or modern comparison point that establishes the gap or parallel[cite: 4, 6]. Date the equivalent.
*   **Pseudoscience Filter:** Verify all scientific claims against peer-reviewed literature[cite: 4, 6].
*   **Counterargument Search:** Search for scholars who dispute the claim and document them[cite: 4, 6].

### 4. Compilation & Script Handoff (Phase 5)
*   Compile all findings into a one-page Research Brief[cite: 6].
*   The brief must contain: approved thesis, 3 strongest pieces of evidence, 2 best expert quotes, Western equivalent gap, best hook ammunition, and the 1 counterargument that must be acknowledged[cite: 4].

### 5. Final Approval
*   The Research Brief and Package must be signed off by the producer before any script generation begins[cite: 4].
