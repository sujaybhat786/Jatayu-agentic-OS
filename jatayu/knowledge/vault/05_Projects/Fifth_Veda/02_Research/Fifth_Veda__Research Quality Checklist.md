# Research Quality Checklist

This checklist acts as a hard gate. No research can move into script generation until every item is verified[cite: 6].

- [ ] Primary source located and logged with exact reference (chapter, verse, page, or archive record)[cite: 6].
- [ ] Minimum peer-reviewed academic validation threshold met (at least 3 papers)[cite: 4, 6].
- [ ] 2-3 credentialed expert voices/quotes identified and logged[cite: 4].
- [ ] Western/modern comparison point identified and accurately dated[cite: 6].
- [ ] Counterargument search completed; any live dispute is either addressed or the thesis is narrowed[cite: 6].
- [ ] Specific numbers, dates, or measurements all trace to logged sources[cite: 6].
- [ ] Two specific evidence beats identified and ranked (one for the Body, one for the Epic Escalation)[cite: 6].
- [ ] One-page Research Brief written and formally approved by the producer[cite: 4, 6].
