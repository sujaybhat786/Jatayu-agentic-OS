# Secondary Sources (Tier 2 & 3)

## Acceptable Secondary Sources
Secondary sources serve as academic validation and translation of primary textual data. 

*   **Peer-Reviewed Work:** Academic translations and journal publications[cite: 6]. These are mandatory for validating scientific claims.
*   **Books & Educational Institutions:** University press books (e.g., Oxford, Cambridge, Columbia University Press) and credentialed-author non-fiction featuring a documented citation trail[cite: 4, 6].

## Expert Voices
Expert voices validate or deepen the argument within scripts[cite: 4]. 
*   **Identification:** Research must identify 2–3 credentialed experts (academics, scientists, historians) who have commented on the specific topic[cite: 4].
*   **Extraction:** Extract actual quotes or paraphrased positions from their published interviews or academic papers[cite: 4].
*   **Context:** Quotes must never be used outside of the documented context in which they were originally made[cite: 6].
