# Research Philosophy

## The Golden Rule of Research
Research is completed and approved before a single line of script is written[cite: 6]. This ordering is structural, not procedural[cite: 6]. Writing a script ahead of its research package inevitably bends the evidence to fit a predetermined emotional narrative[cite: 6]. 

## Evidence-First Mindset
*   **Evidence Before Emotion:** Every claim traces to a primary source, an academic publication, or a documented historical record[cite: 6]. Emotional resonance is the delivery mechanism, never the substitute, for evidence[cite: 6].
*   **Intellectual Honesty:** The research process actively seeks to discover the truth, not merely to validate a previously held cultural belief. 
*   **Truth Over Virality:** If a highly viral, sensational claim cannot be verified through a Tier 1 primary text or a peer-reviewed paper, it is completely banned from production[cite: 5].

## Bias Reduction & Systemic Curiosity
*   **Curiosity Philosophy:** Research should operate as an investigative mechanism. We ask dangerous, highly specific questions to unearth lost or erased systemic knowledge, rather than settling for surface-level trivia. 
*   **Objective Extraction:** Historical evidence—including evidence of colonial-era extraction, destruction, or policy—is presented purely as historical evidence, sourced and documented[cite: 6]. It is never extended into commentary on current-day domestic politics[cite: 6].
