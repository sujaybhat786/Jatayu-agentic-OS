# Fact Verification

## Minimum Evidence Requirements
*   Every specific number, date, name, and measurement appearing in published content must match a logged source from the research package[cite: 6]. 
*   A minimum of 3 academic papers is required for secondary validation[cite: 4]. If fewer than 3 exist, the episode must be reframed[cite: 4].

## Scientific Cross-Checking
*   Every claim involving a scientific mechanism must be checked against peer-reviewed literature[cite: 6].
*   If no peer-reviewed support exists, the claim is qualified in the script or dropped entirely[cite: 6]. 
*   For science-meets-India crossovers, locate the original scientific paper first, then the Indian philosophical parallel, never the reverse[cite: 4].

## Contradictory Evidence & Confidence Levels
Where an academic dispute exists over a specific figure, the script must either state the range with its sourcing or select the most rigorously sourced figure and explicitly note the source[cite: 6]. Research must never simply select the most dramatic figure available[cite: 6].
