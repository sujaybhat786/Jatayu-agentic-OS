# The Fifth Veda: Research Knowledge Pack

## Purpose of this Folder
This directory houses the permanent Research Knowledge Pack for the JATAYU AI Operating System. It defines the absolute standards, hierarchies, and workflows required to execute research for The Fifth Veda. These notes do not contain episode-specific research; they contain the immutable methodology for *how* research is conducted, verified, and structured for cinematic short-form documentary production.

## Relationship with Foundation Documents
The Research pack operates as the execution layer for the principles established in the Foundation Knowledge Pack. While `[[Mission]]` and `[[Vision]]` dictate *why* we build content, and `[[Content Philosophy]]` dictates the format, this folder dictates the evidentiary reality that makes the content defensible. 

## Node Architecture & Reading Order
To properly initialize the research module, process the nodes in the following sequence:

1. **The Mindset:** `[[Fifth_Veda__Research Philosophy]]`
2. **The Rules of Evidence:** `[[Fifth_Veda__Evidence Hierarchy]]`, `[[Fifth_Veda__Primary Sources]]`, `[[Fifth_Veda__Secondary Sources]]`
3. **The Validation Engine:** `[[Fifth_Veda__Fact Verification]]`, `[[Fifth_Veda__Counterargument Policy]]`, `[[Fifth_Veda__Citation Standards]]`
4. **The Execution Pipeline:** `[[Fifth_Veda__Research Workflow]]`, `[[Fifth_Veda__Research Quality Checklist]]`
