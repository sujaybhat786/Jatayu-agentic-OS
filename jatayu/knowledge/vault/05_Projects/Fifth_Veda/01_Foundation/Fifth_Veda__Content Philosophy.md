# Editorial & Production Doctrine

## Short-Form Subtractive Discipline
The Fifth Veda operates on a strict short-form model optimized for vertical 9:16 platforms (Reels, Shorts, TikTok) with runtime envelopes locked between 20 and 60 seconds. The strategy is entirely **subtractive**. The deep research package will always contain infinite data points; execution excellence relies on isolating exactly one primary-source fact and one comparison framework per asset, ruthlessly stripping out secondary details.

## The 4-Beat Short-Form Structural Engine
Every video asset must pass through a fixed, high-retention structural pipeline:


```

┌───────────────────────┐
│  01. ULTIMATE HOOK    │ ◄─── Stops scroll (<3s). Must contain precise number/date/name.
└───────────┬───────────┘
▼
┌───────────────────────┐
│  02. THE BODY SETUP   │ ◄─── Delivers 1 primary source fact + 1 Western comparison point.
└───────────┬───────────┘
▼
┌───────────────────────┐
│  03. PITFALL TWIST    │ ◄─── Drops floor out from viewer assumptions via a trapdoor phrase.
└───────────┬───────────┘
▼
┌───────────────────────┐
│  04. EPIC ESCALATION  │ ◄─── Delivers the "Weapon Line" in total silence. Graphic CTA.
└───────────────────────┘

```

## The Seven Hard Hook Types
Hooks must satisfy the 3-second spoken ceiling rule and feature an implicit or explicit structural tension. The allowed operational variants are:
1.  **The Gap Hook:** States a modern discovery $\rightarrow$ names the western credited figure $\rightarrow$ reveals the earlier Indian textual source $\rightarrow$ drops the calculated year gap.
2.  **The Number Hook:** Leads directly with an unadorned, massive statistic or monetary value (e.g., "$45 Trillion") to create an immediate pattern interrupt.
3.  **The Myth-Bust Hook:** States a popular, soft cultural assumption flatly $\rightarrow$ executes a rapid internal reframe (*"That is not what the text actually says"*).
4.  **The Gateway Hook:** Opens on a universally recognized global object (e.g., Chess) $\rightarrow$ pivots immediately to the undocumented Indian invention origin story.
5.  **The Modern-Problem Hook:** Leads with the viewer's immediate psychological or scientific dead-end (e.g., the hard problem of consciousness) before introducing the ancient cognitive solution.
6.  **The Mundane-Object Hook:** Unpacks a common everyday item (e.g., a spice rack) and instantly maps the ancient pharmacological intelligence layer operating underneath it.
7.  **The POV Hook:** Employs an intimate, family-ancestral narrative bridge that opens outward into a larger historical extraction reality.

## Evidence-First vs. Virality Protocol
The brand explicitly prioritizes **intellectual gravity over cheap algorithmic scale.** If a highly viral, sensational claim cannot be verified through a Tier 1 primary Sanskrit/Pali/Tamil text or a peer-reviewed paper, it is completely banned from production. The system uses sensational hooks, but the payload inside the body must remain entirely bulletproof, sourced, and defensible under academic scrutiny.

## The Google Omni Ingredients Workflow Philosophy
Visual scale leverages a sophisticated two-stage AI production pipeline to maintain high-frequency cinematic execution:
*   **Stage 1: Ingredient Locking:** Every character asset, manuscript texture, and environment archetype is generated, verified against the visual identity, and permanently locked as an anchor reference still before animation begins.
*   **Stage 2: Omni Motion Pass:** Video generation prompts direct the engine to act exclusively on locked Ingredients. This blocks visual drift and guarantees deep cinematic continuity across thousands of programmatic short cuts.

## Technical Visual Guardrails
*   **Pacing Cadence:** Shots are edited to run between 1.5 and 4 seconds maximum. The opening pattern-interrupt shot and the final Weapon Line frame are the only elements allowed to hold to protect cognitive impact.
*   **Visual Anti-Patterns:** Absolute exclusion of cartoon explainer animations, flat corporate vector graphics, tourist-marketing visual saturation, and standard stock-footage montages. 
*   **Attribution Anonymity:** Never use AI engines to generate speculative, imagined faces for historical sages or kings (e.g., Aryabhata, Sushruta). The camera must focus on authentic physical history: archaeological remains, verified stone carvings, real geological landscapes, and palm-leaf manuscripts.
