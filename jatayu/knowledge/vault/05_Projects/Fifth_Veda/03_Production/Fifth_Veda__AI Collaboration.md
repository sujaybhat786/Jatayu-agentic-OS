# AI Collaboration

## Decision Ownership
The integration of AI (JATAYU / Google Omni) is structured to accelerate execution without compromising editorial integrity. The core principle is that AI executes structural formatting and visual generation, while humans own all strategic, factual, and brand-identity decisions[cite: 7].

## AI Responsibilities
*   Execute research gathering against the source hierarchy, surfacing findings for human approval rather than self-approving them[cite: 7].
*   Draft scripts strictly against the 4-beat short-form structural doctrine[cite: 7, 12].
*   Generate and lock visual reference stills (Ingredients)[cite: 7, 12].
*   Generate motion shots against locked references[cite: 7, 12].
*   Run automated fixed-checklist quality passes and flag failures[cite: 7].

## Human Responsibilities
*   Set topic priority and pillar balance across the publishing calendar[cite: 7].
*   Make final judgment calls on content governance edge cases[cite: 7].
*   Own brand-identity decisions and correct visual/tonal drift[cite: 7].

## Human Approval Gates
Four absolute approval gates require human sign-off and may never be fully delegated to AI[cite: 7]:
1.  **The Research Brief:** Must be signed off before scripting begins[cite: 7].
2.  **The Weapon Line:** Must be approved before the rest of the script is finalized[cite: 7].
3.  **Locked Visual Ingredients:** Must be approved before volume motion-shot generation begins[cite: 7].
4.  **The Final Assembled Asset:** Must be approved before publishing[cite: 7].

## Creative Boundaries & Escalation
If the AI flags a topic where the required two-evidence-beat threshold cannot be met, or if counterargument resolution fails, the issue is escalated to the human Creator for reframing[cite: 7, 12]. AI must never silently correct, omit, or fabricate data to force a script to work[cite: 7].
