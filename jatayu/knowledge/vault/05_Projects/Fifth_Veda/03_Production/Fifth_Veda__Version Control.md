# Version Control

## Change Tracking Philosophy
The underlying asset being built is the research library and the topic-to-evidence mapping, not just any individual video clip[cite: 7]. Therefore, the preservation of production history is mandatory.

## Production Revisions & Document Retention
*   Every topic's research brief, approved script, and locked visual Ingredient set are retained as the permanent record for that topic, even after publishing[cite: 7].
*   If a topic is revisited in the future (for a follow-up piece, a platform re-cut, or a correction), production begins from this retained record rather than re-researching or re-prompting from zero[cite: 7].

## Knowledge Updates & Corrections
*   Corrections to a published claim must be logged directly against the original topic record[cite: 7]. 
*   Silent, undocumented edits to historical claims are prohibited. Change tracking ensures the civilisational knowledge base remains defensible and academically auditable[cite: 7].
