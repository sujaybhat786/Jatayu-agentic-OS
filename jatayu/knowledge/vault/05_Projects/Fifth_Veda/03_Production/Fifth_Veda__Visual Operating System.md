# Visual Operating System

## Visual Philosophy
Every frame is built to read as observational documentary footage[cite: 7]. The visual language strictly avoids cartoon illustration, animated explainer styles, tourist-marketing gloss, and 'ancient aliens' aesthetics[cite: 12]. 

## Google Omni Workflow & Ingredient Philosophy
Short-form production runs entirely on Google Omni's Ingredients-to-video pipeline[cite: 12].
*   **Ingredient Locking:** Recurring elements (manuscripts, locations, characters) are generated once as a neutral reference still, approved, and locked as an 'Ingredient'[cite: 12].
*   **AI Visual Consistency:** Every subsequent video prompt references the locked Ingredient by name instead of re-describing it[cite: 12]. 

## Scene Construction & Motion Philosophy
*   **Single Idea Focus:** A scene is built around a single visual idea per shot[cite: 7]. Compound shots are avoided[cite: 7].
*   **Camera Language:** Camera movement is restricted to slow push-ins, slow pull-backs, or static holds[cite: 12]. No camera shake, no whip pans[cite: 12].

## Shot Rhythm
Short-form requires a visual change roughly every 1.5 to 4 seconds to hold retention[cite: 12]. 
*   **Exceptions:** The opening shot holds without a cut to let the pattern-interrupt register, and the final shot holds static and silent for 1-2 seconds before the CTA card[cite: 12].

## Colour Philosophy
*   **Ancient India:** Warm amber-gold colour grade, slightly desaturated, 15–20% filmic grain[cite: 12].
*   **Western/Modern Contrast:** Cool blue-grey colour grade, high contrast, slightly desaturated, 15–20% filmic grain[cite: 12].
*   *Rule:* Never mix suffixes or grades within the same shot[cite: 12].

## Typography Philosophy
*   **Sanskrit Terms/Quotes:** Cormorant Garamond italic, Saffron Gold (#B45309) on dark backgrounds[cite: 12].
*   **Stat Overlays & Titles:** Montserrat Bold (Weight 700), all caps, centered in safe zone[cite: 11, 12].
*   **Body Overlays/Citations:** Inter Regular/Medium, white on dark, or small grey Inter Light for citations in the bottom-left[cite: 11, 12].
