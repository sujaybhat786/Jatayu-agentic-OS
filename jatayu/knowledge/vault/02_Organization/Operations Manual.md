Current  Operational  Bottlenecks  
The  company  currently  faces  the  following  constraints.  
Financial  Resources  
Limited  capital  restricts  hiring  and  rapid  expansion.   
Founder  Bandwidth  
The  Founder  currently  performs  multiple  executive  roles  simultaneously.  This  limits  execution  speed.   
Execution  
Ideas  are  generated  faster  than  they  are  completed.  Execution  discipline  remains  a  priority.   
Sales  
The  company  requires  a  more  consistent  client  acquisition  process.   
Marketing  
Growth  currently  depends  heavily  on  the  Founder.  Future  AI  systems  should  reduce  this  dependency.   
Focus  Management  
The  Founder  has  many  parallel  ideas.  One  of  JATAYU’s  future  responsibilities  is  to  reduce  unnecessary  context  switching  and  
maintain
 
strategic
 
focus.

Project  Classiﬁcation  
Every  initiative  should  belong  to  one  of  six  categories.  
Idea  
A  concept  not  yet  validated.   
Experiment  
An  idea  currently  being  tested.   
Active  Project  
Work  currently  in  progress.  
 
Product  
A  validated  offering  with  ongoing  development.   
Client  Work  
Projects  executed  for  paying  customers.   
Archive  
Completed  or  discontinued  initiatives  retained  for  historical  reference.  This  classification  prevents  the  company  from  confusing  ideas  with  active  commitments.
